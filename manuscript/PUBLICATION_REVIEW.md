5 November 2026. This is a publication-preparation check by the same AI
model, not an additional independent mathematical review or research result.

The typeset PDF has 11 A4 pages. All 11 pages were rendered at 100 dpi and
visually inspected, including the statements, singular-fibre proof, CRT
construction, moving-slab estimate, integer selector, applications, scope
disclosure and bibliography. No clipped equations, missing glyphs or
overfull text boxes were found in the final version.

The 51 displayed mathematical expressions match the revised Markdown
manuscript modulo whitespace; equation labels (1)--(28) occur in sequence
in the source and are present in extracted PDF text. The exported Markdown
also passed the existing document-integrity checker with its unchanged
mathematical expressions and revised theorem/corollary hierarchy.

bibliography-line warning; the relevant rendered page was inspected.

Unicode proof-end squares and a narrow bibliography line. The final source
uses explicit standard TeX font filenames, mathematical square markers and
flexible bibliography line breaking. An intermediate wrapper also failed
while printing a Unicode diagnostic after PDF compilation; this did not
constitute a successful publication check. Failed/superseded local receipts
remain in the original workspace, rather than being represented as passes.

expanded manuscript. Checksums describe file integrity, not correctness.
